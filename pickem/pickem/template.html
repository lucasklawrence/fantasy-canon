Do the flags work?

For each kind of flag that argued against the favorite: how often the favorite actually lost, against how often the market expected it to. A positive edge means the flag found upsets the market missed. Small samples swing wildly, so wait for 20+ games before trusting one.

Market is the DraftKings moneyline with the bookmaker margin removed. FPI is ESPN's own win model. Starter means at least 60% of snaps in their best 3 games this season (nflverse snap counts). NEW means the player was on the field last week, so the market may still be catching up.

Practice participation appears once teams file Wednesday reports. Lines and injury statuses change through the week, so regenerate Thursday and again Sunday morning.

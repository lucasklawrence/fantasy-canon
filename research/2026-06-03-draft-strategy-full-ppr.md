# 2026 Fantasy Football Draft Report
### 12-team snake · redraft · full-PPR

## ⚡ Takeaway
There is **no consensus 1.01** this year — FFC's full-PPR board has Ja'Marr Chase No. 1, while FantasyPros, ESPN (Mike Clay), and PFF put Bijan Robinson / Jahmyr Gibbs at RB1/RB2 with Chase as the top WR. Draft by tiers, not by a single ranking.

## 1. Pick-order & positional strategy `high`
- Elite tier is three players: **Chase (WR), Robinson (RB), Gibbs (RB)**; the 1.01 is board-dependent.
  - FFC (full-PPR): Chase 1.4 → Robinson 1.8 → Barkley 2.1
  - FantasyPros: Robinson 1.0 → Gibbs 2.0 → Chase 3.0
  - Draft Sharks 3D value (format-aware): **Puka Nacua #1 overall** in full-PPR (target volume is king).
- Round 1 shape: ~6 of top 12 are RBs (Robinson, Gibbs, Taylor, McCaffrey, Cook, Jeanty), but 3 WRs go top-5 → value/tier-driven, not dogmatic.
- At the turn (10–12): the long wait justifies reaching for tier breaks (e.g., Drake Maye pick 40 vs ~70s ADP). Balanced early build observed (16 RB / 16 WR / 2 TE / 2 QB through 3 rounds) — but that mock was half-PPR; full-PPR tilts a bit more WR.

## 2. Player values `high`
**Top targets** — RB: Robinson, Gibbs, McCaffrey (then Taylor, Achane). WR: Nacua, Chase, Smith-Njigba.
**Sleepers/breakouts** — Bhayshul Tuten (RB, JAX), Kenneth Gainwell (RB, TB), Tyler Shough (QB, NO, ~QB20), Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR, ~13th-rd dart).
**Avoids/busts** — Christian McCaffrey (age 30 / 4 games in 2024 / Mike Evans target competition at RB3 ADP), Bucky Irving (shoulder surgery, YAC/att 3.93→2.62, committee), Sam Darnold (QB20, run-first SEA, lost Walker).
> McCaffrey appears as both a top target and an avoid — that's the real market split.

## 3. Roster construction & handcuffs `high`
- **Zero-RB is conditional, not a creed** — pivot to a workhorse RB if rivals load WRs through round 2.
- Elite handcuffs (only two): **Blake Corum** (LAR), **Zach Charbonnet** (SEA — ⚠️ torn ACL, may miss start of 2026; ranking "assumes health").
- Tier-2 handcuffs w/ standalone value: Brian Robinson Jr. (ATL), Ray Davis (BUF), Tyler Allgeier (ARI).

## 4. In-draft / advanced tactics `medium–high`
- Draft by tiers, watch for tier cliffs, read the room at the turn, save late rounds for high-ceiling fliers (Brooks, Shough).

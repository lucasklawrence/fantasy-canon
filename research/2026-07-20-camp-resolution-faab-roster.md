## Part 1 — Seattle backfield

**The lead-back question is genuinely unresolved, and that's the honest answer.** `confidence: high · 3-0`
No Seattle camp practice reporting exists yet. The most recent substantive item is July 2 — Charbonnet doing light work on a side field at June minicamp, with the knee checkup still in _future tense_. A targeted re-fetch for anything newer returned "Items dated after July 2, 2026: None."

**But the market gap is real and correctly measured**, and it's wider than it looks:

| Player          | Rank | ADP   | StdDev | Times drafted | Round |
| --------------- | ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------- | ----- |
| Jadarian Price  | 76   | 73.6  | 8.3    | 321           | 7     |
| Zach Charbonnet | 182  | 155.7 | 19.3   | **70**        | 13    |

**The load-bearing detail: Charbonnet was drafted in only 70 of 2,448 mocks — he went undrafted ~97% of the time.** His 155.7 is conditional on being selected at all, so his true market value is _worse_ than 155.7, and the real gap exceeds 82 picks. His standard deviation is 2.3× Price's: the market is both bearish and deeply uncertain. Practically, he's closer to a waiver-wire name than a Round 13 pick.

**ESPN's depth chart misled us.** It lists Charbonnet as starter, but with a "(Q)" flag — and ESPN depth charts are **auto-generated projections, not official designations.** The same trap hit Kyler Murray in Minnesota. This is the origin of the "80-pick arbitrage" I flagged yesterday; it was largely an artifact of trusting an auto-generated chart.

**Kenneth Walker III — fully resolved.** `confidence: high · 3-0` He's a Chief. chiefs.com lists him (#9, RB) and seahawks.com does not list him at all. **His ADP 22.5 was genuine data, not a rank-column artifact** — verified via neighbors that diverge from their ranks (Chase Brown 16.3 at rank 18, Barkley 19.7 at rank 20).

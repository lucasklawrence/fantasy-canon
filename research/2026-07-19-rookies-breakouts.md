# 2026 Rookies & Breakout Candidates

### 12-team snake · redraft · full-PPR · ADP as of mid-July 2026

## ⚡ Takeaway

Exactly one 2026 rookie is priced as a real early-round asset — Cardinals RB **Jeremiyah Love** (~22.6 ADP in 12-team PPR) — and after him there is a **~39-pick cliff with no 2026 rookie in it**. Every other rookie is a Round 5+ dart throw, so spend Rounds 3-6 on the _second-year_ 2025 class, which has established roles and is priced ahead of the rookies for good reason.

---

## Rookies — the entire draftable set

`confidence: high`

**Jeremiyah Love (RB, ARI) — ~22.6 FFC 12-team PPR / pick 15 Underdog / RB13-14.**
No. 3 overall NFL pick (picks 1-2 were QB Fernando Mendoza and EDGE David Bailey, making Love definitionally the highest-capital rookie RB), signed 4yr/$53M — the most guaranteed money ever for a running back. ADP verified two independent ways: Underdog's raw embedded JSON (pick 15, 2.03, created 2026-07-13) and Fantasy Football Calculator's 12-team PPR board sampled from 2,415 mocks July 12-19 — that FFC figure **explicitly clears our known stale-data caveat** on that site. Depth chart corroborated at ESPN, RotoWire, DraftSharks and azcardinals.com.

ESPN's Mike Clay carries the bull case: "each of the past nine running backs drafted in the top 12 went on to finish top 12 in fantasy points at the position as a rookie," averaging 289.1 touches and 17.3 fantasy PPG. Treat that as weaker than it sounds — it's streak-framed and carries a selection effect, since top-12 RB capital mostly proxies for a pre-committed bellcow role rather than independently predicting one.

**The live debate is touch share, not role.** `confidence: high`
Even the bullish SI ranking hedges that Love "will come right in and be the top back in Arizona (even if he does lose some work to Tyler Allgeier and James Conner)." FantasyPros' Evan Tarracciano flatly fades: "Love is currently being drafted as the RB14, ahead of more proven options like Breece Hall, Kyren Williams and Bucky Irving. That is way too rich for my tastes." The committee is not speculation — Allgeier signed with Arizona (2yr/$12.25M) on March 12. FantasyLife, RotoBaller and Footballguys all project 45-55% of RB touches rather than 65%+, noting Allgeier took 31 red-zone carries to Bijan Robinson's 32 in Atlanta and that Arizona ranked 30th in red-zone attempts. One thing the fade overstates: FantasyPros itself calls Love "the best slot receiving option on the team" — the cap is on target competition, not his receiving ability.

**Then the cliff.** `confidence: high`
On Underdog's raw dataset the top 50 contains exactly one 2026 rookie. On FFC's 12-team PPR board every player between Love (22.6) and Carnell Tate (61.5) is a veteran or prior-class player — a ~39-pick window with no 2026 rookie. Two separately-conducted CBS post-draft expert mocks independently reproduced it: first rookie off the board was Love in Round 3, **zero rookies in Rounds 1-2**.

Price's slot is the least robust number here — Underdog has 57, FFC has 73.8, a 17-pick spread. Cite him as "Round 6-ish, wide source variance," not 5.09. Also: FFC's own round.pick column is internally inconsistent (it appears to be a modal-pick field, not derived from ADP) — derive round.pick from ADP yourself.

**Jadarian Price is the best value bet after Love — but check camp first.** `confidence: medium`
Opportunity is structural: Kenneth Walker III left for Kansas City, Zach Charbonnet tore his ACL in the Jan. 17 divisional round (surgery Feb. 20) — roughly 250 vacated touches, and Price was the 32nd overall pick. FantasyPros projects "15-18 touches per week out of the gate"; CBS's Eisenberg would draft him "as early as Round 5." **The catch:** ESPN's Brady Henderson reports Price is _not_ the back working most often with the first team — that's George Holani — and Seattle's own site ran "Does Rookie Jadarian Price Win The Starting Job?" Charbonnet is also progressing toward a realistic mid-October return. This is the single biggest unresolved risk in the rookie tier.

**Carnell Tate — trusted sources split.** `confidence: medium`
FantasyPros calls WR24 "just about right," arguing he leads Tennessee ahead of Wan'Dale Robinson and Calvin Ridley. Eisenberg reaches the opposite practical conclusion: "I'd rather wait for Lemon or Tyson" — same tier, two-plus rounds cheaper. That's a wait-for-cheaper-equivalent take, not a fade. Two counterweights FantasyPros underweights: Wan'Dale Robinson signed 4yr/$70M ($38M guaranteed) reuniting with Brian Daboll, now Titans OC — not a slot-specialist contract; and Tennessee finished 30th in scoring and passing yards in 2025.

## Breakout candidates — 2nd and 3rd year

`confidence: high` for Pierce and Singleton, `medium` for the rest

**Alec Pierce (WR, IND, 4th yr) — ~7.08 ADP.** The strongest documented volume-vacancy case, and the one with the biggest hole in it. PFF (July 8) carries the figures: 17.4% target rate, 20.1 aDOT, WR27 finish, and ~125 vacated Colts targets after Michael Pittman Jr. was traded to Pittsburgh (March 13) and Adonai Mitchell went to the Jets. The arithmetic checks (Pittman's 111 + Mitchell's ~14). **Two flags PFF omits:** Pierce had left ankle surgery in March with a self-reported four-to-six-month rehab (reported June 10) that could cost camp and early games — and the market has _not_ repriced him. And PFF says those targets went "to the receiver room," not to Pierce; Josh Downs (23% target rate, 7.49 aDOT) is the better structural fit for Pittman's short-area work, with Tyler Warren also competing. ESPN's Stephen Holder projects a more modest "eight to 10" targets per game.

**Emeka Egbuka (WR, TB, 2nd yr) — NFFC 30 / Underdog 44.** The market's leading second-year riser, 37.8 → 32.9 mid-June to mid-July. But RotoWire's Donabedian is explicitly agnostic on why: "There's no specific news nugget or report driving it," floating new OC Zac Robinson optimism, "organic momentum," or podcast/Twitter hype — and he undercuts his own Atlanta analogy. **Use NFFC 30 for redraft; 32.9 is best-ball.** Note the move is ~0.41 rounds (early-4th to late-3rd), not "a full round."

**Tre Harris (WR, LAC, 2nd yr) — 14.08 Underdog, hard ceiling at Round 12.** 4for4: "a nice add to the Justin Herbert-led offense... as long as he doesn't creep up any higher than the 12th round." That last clause is a price ceiling, not a footnote — pass if he's going earlier.

**Adonai Mitchell (WR, NYJ, 3rd yr) — 15.05 Underdog / FantasyPros 172, WR65.** The largest Underdog riser of the last four weeks (-32.7 picks, roughly double the next-largest mover). 4for4 likes him as "a WR4/5 with upside." Qualifier: the support is spring coachspeak from Aaron Glenn and Geno Smith — a drafter-popularity signal, not a snap or target signal.

**Nick Singleton (RB, TEN) — fade.** `confidence: high` Fell 22.6 picks to a best-ball 17th-round ADP, sits third behind Tony Pollard and Tyjae Spears, recovering from a Jones fracture (Senior Bowl practice, late January; surgery in Birmingham, missed Combine testing). Two corrections: the surgery did _not_ cause the June-July ADP drop — 4for4 attributes that to landing spot and draft capital. And **a 17.12 ADP only exists in 18-round best ball** — in our 15-16 round redraft he's a waiver-wire name, not a pick.

## In-draft tactics

`confidence: medium` — synthesized from the verified ADP structure, not from any single source's prescription

- **Rounds 2-3:** Love is the only rookie in range. Take him if the committee discount pushes him past pick 24; pass if your room prices him ahead of Breece Hall, Kyren Williams and Bucky Irving.
- **Rounds 3-6:** where second-year value actually clusters. The Underdog top-50 sweep showed the 2025 class (Jeanty 13, Hampton 18, McMillan 37, Loveland 39, Warren 43, Egbuka 44, Judkins 51) priced well ahead of the 2026 class. Egbuka at NFFC 30 sits in this band.
- **Round 7:** Alec Pierce is the highest-conviction volume play — discounted for ankle risk.
- **Rounds 5-9:** rookie WRs. Eisenberg explicitly prefers waiting for Lemon (~R8) and Tyson (~R7-8) over paying for Tate (~R5).
- **Rounds 12-14:** Tre Harris (ceiling Round 12) and Adonai Mitchell.
- **Undrafted in this format:** Nick Singleton.

**Governing principle** (RotoWire, July 17): "Draft capital and talent matter... but immediate opportunity often drives rookie fantasy value more than the player's skill." Note this _subordinates_ draft capital, it does not dismiss it — and the stronger corollary that late-capital RBs systematically outproduce early-capital ones was **refuted 0-3**, which matters because it would otherwise argue against the Love thesis.

## 🔴 Caveats

1. **Format mismatch is the single biggest hazard here.** Much of the cleanest ADP data (DraftSharks/Underdog, 4for4, RotoWire best-ball analysis, BB10, Drafters) is **best ball**, which systematically inflates high-variance pass-catchers and rookies. Both CBS expert mocks are **half-PPR**, which understates rookie WR cost relative to our full-PPR league. Only the FFC 12-team PPR board and the NFFC figures are natively in our format — those override any Underdog pick number cited above.
2. **Vintage.** The FantasyPros "8 Rookies to Draft or Fade" piece and both CBS mocks are May 2026, pre-camp, ~2 months stale. Their positional ranks have already drifted (Price went RB24 → RB26 on FFC). Rookie ADP historically climbs through August camp and preseason — **re-verify every figure before a late-August draft.**
3. **Underpriced injury risk.** Pierce's March ankle surgery is absent from the PFF article that is the primary source for his case, and the market hasn't repriced him. Clearest information gap in the dataset, sitting directly on the highest-conviction breakout call.
4. **Verification gaps.** Several verifiers exhausted the session WebSearch budget before completing adversarial cross-checks, so the 4for4 Harris ADP, RotoWire Egbuka figures, FantasyPros May rankings and DraftSharks Underdog numbers are single-sourced. The DraftSharks page truncated on both fetches — those Underdog numbers are verified by proxy through the raw JSON layer, not the rendered page.
5. **Source hygiene.** FantasyPros' 2026 PPR consensus currently aggregates only **three** app-native sources (BB10, Sleeper, Drafters, all dated 7/18) — no ESPN, Yahoo, NFFC or CBS. Sleeper is a major home-league redraft platform, so the defensible takeaway is the narrow one (no ESPN/Yahoo/CBS/NFFC), not "it's all best ball." Separately, the widely-linked ESPN 2026 rankings hub URL returns **zero player data** — the numbers live on leaf pages (e.g. `/id/48711830`). Never cite the hub for a valuation.

## Open questions / follow-ups

- Does Pierce's ankle cost him camp or games, and does 7.08 correct once camp reports land?
- Who actually leads Seattle's backfield Week 1? Price's RB24-26 price assumes a lead role he hasn't won — Holani is taking first-team reps.
- What is Love's real touch share? Projections span 45-55% to bellcow. That single number decides whether 22.6 is value or trap.
- Does the rookie WR tier reprice upward through August? The "wait for Lemon/Tyson" tactic depends on ADP staying put, and full-PPR argues for earlier costs than the half-PPR mocks implied.

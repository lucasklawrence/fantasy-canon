# 2026 Build Templates: Zero-RB vs Hero-RB vs Robust-RB
### 12-team snake · redraft · full-PPR

## ⚡ Takeaway
Three concrete, verified redraft templates exist for 2026 — but the evidence tilts **toward RB-forward builds (Hero-RB / Robust-RB)** this year: early-RB hit rates ran 70–75% in 2025, Zero-RB's contrarian edge has eroded as WR-early went mainstream, and full-PPR scarcity data (13 RBs vs 10 WRs at 15+ PPR/gm) favors locking RBs early.

## 🥇 Hero-RB (best-documented for 2026) `high`
**Shape:** one elite RB in R1 (or R2) → hammer WRs R2–4 → splurge TE/QB ~R4–5 → fill RB2 from R6+.
- **R1 RB anchors by slot:** early — Gibbs / Bijan Robinson; mid — Taylor / McCaffrey; late — James Cook / Jeanty.
- **R2–3 WR targets (if RB in R1):** Drake London, Nico Collins, George Pickens, Olave, McMillan, Zay Flowers.
- **Verified executed mock (late slot):** Cook 1.09 → Collins 2.04 → McConkey 3.09 … QB punted to Stafford 13.09.

## 🥈 Robust-RB `high`
**Shape:** RBs with your first 2–3 picks (≥3 RBs inside R4–5) → load WR depth R6–10 → split-backfield RBs as flex (Dowdle, Monangai, Gainwell, Corum).
- **Slot rule:** early pick → Robinson/Gibbs ("closest things to a guarantee," ADP 1.5); late first → **James Cook over Achane/Jeanty** (three straight top-12 PPR finishes). McCaffrey/Taylor are the mid-slot anchors. (Jeanty omission was a 2-1 vote — a defensible safety call, not consensus.)

## 🥉 Zero-RB (verified but weakest 2026 case) `high template / eroding thesis`
**Verified round-by-round (FantasyPros 1.04 mock):** R1 Smith-Njigba → R2 Collins → R3 Josh Allen → R4 Jameson Williams → R5 McConkey → R6 Fannin Jr (TE) → **first RB R7 Montgomery** → R8 Dobbins → R9 Aaron Jones → R10 Rachaad White.
- **Evidence against it this year (3-0):** FTN — 14/20 top-PPG RBs had top-20 ADP (70%); 9/12 top-12 ADP RBs finished top-12 (75%). DraftSharks — Zero-RB's popularity "closed the opportunity gap." FantasyPros — "the pendulum might finally be swinging back toward running backs."
- Single-mock test (FFToday, n=1, low weight): Zero-RB graded B-, finished 6th, made playoffs.

## 🎯 Choosing by snake slot `high`
- **Picks 1–3:** take the RB (Robinson/Gibbs) → sets up Hero-RB or RB/RB; pivoting WR opens the Zero-RB path. Dual-RB RB2 options: Hampton, Love, Henry.
- **Picks 5–8:** Taylor/McCaffrey anchor the Hero-RB/Robust builds.
- **Picks 10–12 (turn):** Cook/Jeanty as the late-R1 anchor; the verified late-slot mock is the Hero-RB execution above. ⚠️ No verified **turn-specific Zero-RB** build was found — open question.
- WR-WR-WR is "safer" only in the depth sense (more viable early WRs; depth persists R6–10) — a 2025 PFF *principle*; its concrete template was **refuted 0-3**.

# 2026 Redraft Full-PPR Rankings — Top Board & Positional Tiers
### 12-team snake · redraft · full-PPR · data as of July 5–12, 2026

## ⚡ Takeaway
The elite core is stable — **RBs Robinson / Gibbs / McCaffrey** and **WRs Nacua / Chase / Smith-Njigba** — but the **1.01 and the round-one RB/WR balance are source-dependent**, so the single most valuable prep step is knowing which board your league platform defaults to. **Wait on QB** (first one, Josh Allen, doesn't go until ~pick 29) and **grab one of the two elite TEs (McBride or Bowers) in rounds 2–3** before a steep cliff.

## The 1.01 & the elite tier
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0)
- The 1.01 is a **genuine three-way, source-dependent debate**, but the top five names are consistent across every board: **Bijan Robinson (ATL), Jahmyr Gibbs (DET), Christian McCaffrey (SF), Puka Nacua (LAR), Ja'Marr Chase (CIN)**.
- **FantasyFootballCalculator ADP** and **ESPN/Field Yates** open **Bijan–Gibbs** (FFC: Bijan 1.6, Gibbs 2.0, McCaffrey 4.8).
- **DraftSharks' ESPN 1-PPR ADP** flips it to **Gibbs (1.1) – Robinson (1.2)** – McCaffrey (1.5).
- **Field Yates** goes RB-RB-RB: Robinson, Gibbs, McCaffrey at 1.01–1.03.
- **FantasyPros ECR is the outlier** — **Ja'Marr Chase as WR1 at 1.01**, then Robinson, Nacua, Gibbs, Smith-Njigba (all Tier 1).
- *Divergence is only at the ordinal 1.01; the tier itself is settled.*

## First-round RB/WR balance — the thing to check before you draft
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0)
- **The round-one RB/WR split swings hard by source** — this drives whether the value falls to you at your slot:
  - **FantasyPros ECR: WR-heavy, 8 WR / 4 RB** in the top 12 (RBs only at Robinson #2, Gibbs #4, McCaffrey #7, Jonathan Taylor #10). Top 12: Chase, Robinson, Nacua, Gibbs, Smith-Njigba, St. Brown, McCaffrey, Lamb, Jefferson, Taylor, London, Nico Collins.
  - **ESPN / Field Yates: RB-heavy** (~9 RB / 6 WR in the top 15). Picks 7–15: Taylor, Achane, St. Brown, Cook, Henry, Jefferson, Lamb, Chase Brown, Jacobs.
  - **DraftSharks ESPN ADP: even 6 RB / 6 WR.** Round 1: Gibbs, Robinson, Nacua, Chase, McCaffrey, Smith-Njigba, St. Brown, Taylor, Lamb, Achane, Jefferson, Ashton Jeanty.
- **Actionable:** on a WR-heavy platform, elite RBs slide toward you; on an RB-heavy one, WRs do. Know your platform's default board before draft day.

## WR tiers
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0)
- **Tier 1 "PPR MVPs" (Draft Sharks):** **Puka Nacua** and **Ja'Marr Chase**, both projected for **180+ targets** — a clear tier above the rest.
  - Nacua-vs-Chase for WR1 is a legitimate coin flip: **Field Yates ranks Nacua WR1** (#4 overall, ahead of Chase #5); **PFF corroborates Nacua** on an NFL-best 96.3 receiving grade over two seasons; **FantasyPros ranks Chase WR1** (#1 overall).
- **Just behind:** **Jaxon Smith-Njigba** (WR3) and **Amon-Ra St. Brown** (WR4), then Lamb, Jefferson, London, Nico Collins.

## TE — two elite, then a cliff
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0)
- **Tier 1 (Draft Sharks: "TEs in name, WR1s in production"):** **Trey McBride** and **Brock Bowers** — both late-2nd / early-3rd (DraftSharks ESPN ADP: McBride 2.7, Bowers 2.8; FFC first TE = McBride at overall 32 / ADP 29.3). McBride-vs-Bowers is a platform-dependent coin flip.
- **Steep cliff after them:** next TE (Tyler Warren ~4.7, Sam LaPorta ~6.7, George Kittle ~6.11, Colston Loveland) falls roughly two rounds later (~pick 46+).
- **Tactic:** either draft McBride/Bowers in rounds 2–3, **or punt TE entirely** and wait for the streaming tier — don't get caught in the dead zone between.

## QB — wait, and prioritize legs
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0)
- **The position is exceptionally deep** — the first QB (**Josh Allen**) doesn't leave the board until ~**pick 29–31** (FFC ADP 28.8).
- **Allen is the consensus QB1, alone in his own tier** (Yahoo "The One of One"). Elite tier behind him: **Lamar Jackson, Drake Maye, Jalen Hurts, Joe Burrow, Jayden Daniels** (4for4 order: Allen, Jackson, Maye, Daniels, Hurts, Burrow, Herbert, Lawrence, Prescott, C. Williams).
- **Prioritize mobile/rushing QBs** for the **~4–5 FPPG premium** (a QB with ~500 rush yds + 5 rush TD averages ~4.7 more FPPG than a pocket passer). Draft Sharks QB Tier 1 = Allen, Jackson.

## Buy candidate & injury watch
`confidence: medium`
- **BUY — TreVeyon Henderson (RB, NE)** `vote 3-0`: Justin Boone tags him a "strong buy" and "one of the most obvious 2026 breakout targets with a true high-end RB1 ceiling if/when he takes over," with **Rhamondre Stevenson only an "RB3/flex, but the clock is ticking."** Henderson out-produced Stevenson per game in 2025 (53.6 vs 52.4 rush yds/gm) and was a top-10 back over the final eight weeks. *Caveat: this is a role-change projection, not a settled depth chart — RotoBaller still gives Stevenson the "inside track" to open camp. July reports have him ~21 weeks post-op, running 16+ mph and doing cutting drills with "no setbacks," and there's no PUP/IR designation. *But the timeline is aggressive for a ~32-year-old Achilles — some outlets call Week 1 a game-time decision. Treat as a discount-buy with a handcuff, not set-and-forget.*

## 🔴 Caveats
1. **Source divergence is the central finding, not noise.** The #1 overall differs by source (FFC/ESPN → Bijan; DraftSharks → Gibbs; FantasyPros → Chase), and round-one RB/WR balance ranges from 8-4 WR-heavy (FantasyPros) to 6-6 (DraftSharks) to RB-heavy (ESPN). Know which board your platform uses.
2. **Time-sensitive / live snapshot.** All ADP is from the **July 5–12, 2026** window and drifts daily — the Bijan-vs-Gibbs 1.01 flip and Nacua-vs-Chase WR1 will shift. **Re-pull the day of your draft.**
3. **Some "primary" tiers are one expert's board**, not consensus (Draft Sharks tiers, Field Yates top-160, Yahoo QB tiers explicitly labeled "preliminary"). Weight accordingly.
4. **Notation mixing:** DraftSharks/FFC quotes blend round.pick (e.g. "2.7" = round 2, pick 7) with overall ADP. Both were reconciled here, but watch for it in raw sources.
5. **Injury risk:** Kittle's "on-track" status is the softest datapoint (2-1 vote, aggressive Achilles timeline). Also flagged in the raw research but not adversarially verified: Patrick Mahomes (ACL rehab), Malik Nabers (secondary knee procedure, behind schedule) — verify before drafting.

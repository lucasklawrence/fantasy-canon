# 2026 Mid-Round Tiers, Rookie Pecking Order & Fade List
### 12-team snake · redraft · full-PPR · follow-up to the 2026-07-12 top-board report

## ⚡ Takeaway
Build an RB/WR foundation early and **stream QB and TE late**. RB reliability falls off a cliff after Round 7, so target backs in the Round 4–7 band; WR is **shallower than it looks** (draft receivers aggressively rounds 4–6). Big correction: the hyped "rookie" RBs **Jeanty, Hampton, Henderson are 2025 draftees in year two, not 2026 rookies** — the only true rookie worth an early pick is **Jeremiyah Love (ARI)**.

> ⚠️ **Correction to the top-board report** (`2026-07-12-2026-redraft-ppr-rankings.md`): that report called Ashton Jeanty a "rookie" and grouped Jeanty/Hampton/Henderson as "elite rookies." Adversarial verification here (3-0) confirms they were **2025** draftees — treat them as second-year players, not rookie lottery tickets.

## Mid-round RB tiers & the "dead zone" (Rds 4–8)
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0)
- **Round 4–5 (RB2/RB3):** **Quinshon Judkins, Breece Hall, David Montgomery** — "clearly ahead" — then **D'Andre Swift** and **Bucky Irving**, both carrying real backfield-split risk (Swift splits with Kyle Monangai; Irving splits with up to two backs). *(CBS / Dave Richard)*
- **Round 6–7 (Tier 5 — the last startable-RB tier):** **TreVeyon Henderson, Bhayshul Tuten, Chuba Hubbard, Tony Pollard, Jaylen Warren**, plus Price and Croskey-Merritt. Richard calls this "the last tier where you might pull a reliable starter."
- **After Round 7:** you're drafting committee backs hoping for a ~10 half-PPR average. **Don't chase RB there** — you should already have your backs.
- **Actionable:** load your RB2/RB3 in the Round 4–7 band; the reliability cliff is real and steep.

## Mid-round WR tiers — draft aggressively
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0)
- **WR is shallower than commonly believed** (Pat Fitzmaurice, FantasyPros, Jul 8): *"Some fantasy managers believe the WR position is deep. They are wrong."* Late-round WR hits are rare because an injured receiver's targets scatter. **Build your WR corps aggressively in the mid-rounds (picks ~50–75) before supply dries up.**
- **WR2/WR3 value targets:**
  - **FantasyPros Tier 4 "Strong WR2 Targets With Room to Climb":** **Zay Flowers (BAL), Ladd McConkey (LAC)** — "stable enough for lineups while still carrying upside."
  - **DraftSharks WR Tier 4** (Flowers, Chris Olave, Malik Nabers, DeVonta Smith, **Tee Higgins**, Garrett Wilson, **Jaylen Waddle**) — "at worst quality WR2s and at best WR1s." **Best values: Tee Higgins** (~late Rd 3) and **Jaylen Waddle** (~late Rd 4 / pick 48).

## QB — wait and stream
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0)
- Spend early picks on RB/WR; **wait on QB**. Josh Allen carries a **late-3rd ADP (#34)** while **Brock Purdy sits at a late-8th ADP (#95)** — Fitzmaurice would rather have a **Purdy/McConkey combo than an Allen/Michael-Wilson combo.**
- **Streaming works:** ~1.8 QBs per week finish top-5 despite not being top-15 preseason. **Purdy + Baker Mayfield** is flagged as one of the strongest committee pairings, both draftable outside the top-12 QBs. *(Standard 1QB — not Superflex.)*

## TE — wait and stream from the Round 6–7 turn
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0)
- After the two elite TEs (McBride/Bowers, covered in the top-board report), the mid-round streaming tier is:
  - **Tucker Kraft** (~6th; was pacing as a fantasy TE2 when he tore his ACL in 2025, tracking for Week 1 2026 — health-dependent),
  - **Harold Fannin** (~6th/7th turn; 72-731-6 rookie line in 2025),
  - **Kyle Pitts** (~7th; TE2 in half-PPR in 2025).

## Rookie pecking order (read the correction)
`confidence: high` (vote 3-0, except where noted)
- **Jeremiyah Love (RB, ARI, No. 3 overall) — the one true 2026 rookie to target. BUY.** FantasyPros: "should get drafted as a top-12 running back" and "far and away the top-scoring rookie RB." First true rookie off the board (~Round 2, RB9–RB13, ADP ~18–23). Market has him at RB13 while his projection is top-12 — that one-slot gap is the value.
- **"Rookies" who are actually second-year (2025 draftees):**
  - **Ashton Jeanty (LV)** — only rookie to finish RB1 in 2025 (RB11 PPR); Dave Richard's Round-2 tier.
  - **Omarion Hampton (LAC)** — RB13 on a per-game basis in 2025 (missed time, ankle); Round-2 tier.
  - **TreVeyon Henderson (NE)** — RB21 in 2025, week-to-week inconsistent; falls to the Round 6–7 tier (big gap below the other two). *(This is the same Henderson flagged as a breakout BUY in the top-board report — the upside case still holds; just not as a "rookie.")*
- **Tetairoa McMillan (WR, CAR)** `vote 2-1` — mid-tier **WR2-range**, not elite. FantasyPros WR Tier 3 "Upside and Projection" (with George Pickens): "a high-volume WR2 with room for more." Also played in 2025 (Bryce Young), so not a true rookie either. Value contingent on role growth.
- **Rookie tier placements** `confidence: medium` (vote 2-1 — dissent was on the "rookie" label, not the data): Richard slots Jeanty & Hampton in the Round-2 tier, Love in the Round-3 tier, Henderson down in the Round 6–7 tier.

## Fade / avoid list (adversarially confirmed only)
`confidence: high unless noted`
- **Bucky Irving (RB, TB)** `vote 3-0` — overvalued at **ADP 44 / RB20**. Poor efficiency (51st of 58 qualified backs in PFF rush grade in 2025), **zero carries inside the 5-yard line**, and pass-down competition from Kenneth Gainwell. Also the split-risk half of CBS's Round 4–5 tier. *(Ted Chmyz, FantasyPros)*
- **Colston Loveland (TE, CHI)** `vote 3-0` — caution at **ADP 33 / TE3**. Target competition from Luther Burden III and Rome Odunze isn't priced into his late-3rd cost (Burden out-gained him 2.83 to 2.00 yards per route). Boom/bust median. *(ADP has since drifted toward ~40–46 / Round 4, but TE3 holds.)*
- **Christian McCaffrey (RB, SF)** `confidence: medium` — **softer fade.** Went pick 3 in ESPN's post-draft mock (over Nacua, which "raised eyebrows"), and the age-30 / heavy-2025-workload skepticism is real — **but two stronger DraftSharks-based McCaffrey fades were REFUTED in verification**, so this is a lean, not a firm avoid.

## Early-build market signal
`confidence: medium` (vote 3-0 on the facts; caveat below)
- ESPN's post-draft mock ("RB-RB is back") had **RBs outpace WRs 15-13 through the first 30 picks**, with **six straight RBs in Round 2** — supporting securing RB early before the Round 6–7 cliff. *Caveat: it's a 10-team mock, so treat the round math as directional.* This sits in genuine tension with Fitzmaurice's WR-aggressive rounds 4–6 advice. **Practical read: secure both RB and WR before Round 7–8, then stream QB/TE.**

## 🔴 Caveats
1. **Rookie labeling is the headline correction** — Jeanty, Hampton, Henderson (and McMillan) all played in 2025; only Jeremiyah Love is a true 2026 rookie. Frame accordingly.
2. **Single-analyst opinions, not hard facts.** Nearly all tier/fade findings rest on one accurately-attributed analyst's board (DraftSharks/Smola, CBS/Richard, FantasyPros/Fitzmaurice/Chmyz). High confidence the source *says* it; the underlying rankings are one view.
3. **WR-depth thesis is contested** — Zero-RB analysts argue the opposite. The RB-early vs. WR-aggressive-mid-round advice are in real tension (resolve by securing both before Round 7–8).
4. **Round math mixes formats** — some sources are 10-team mocks (ESPN), some 12-team full-PPR (DraftSharks/FantasyPros/CBS), some half-PPR finishes (Pitts/Fannin TE ranks). Convert for our 12-team 1.0-PPR league.
5. **Time-sensitive ADP** — Loveland drifting 33→~40-46; Love shown at pick 14 in one mock vs. ~pick 23 market. Snapshots, not fixed. **Re-pull before your draft.**
6. **Six fade/rookie claims were refuted** — see the killed list; don't resurrect them.

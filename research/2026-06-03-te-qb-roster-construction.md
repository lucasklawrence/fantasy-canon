# 2026 TE & QB Landscape + Roster Construction
### 12-team snake · redraft · full-PPR (follow-up)

## ⚡ Takeaway
TE and QB are now clear: **two elite TEs (McBride, Bowers) then a cliff — grab one early or wait into the double digits**; **Josh Allen QB1 but wait at QB** (rounds 5–8 or two late) given a deep middle tier. The round-by-round Zero-RB/Hero-RB/Robust-RB **build templates could not be verified** — that gap remains open.

## Tight End `high`
- **Elite Tier 1 (only two):** **Trey McBride** and **Brock Bowers** — both early-2nd-round picks (Underdog ADP ~20–25). Clear gap to the field. (TE1-vs-TE2 ordering varies by source; Bowers first on DraftSharks.)
- **Young Tier 2:** **Colston Loveland** (CHI, TE3) and **Tyler Warren** (IND, TE4, off a 76-817-4 rookie year) — ahead of veterans like LaPorta.
- **Why early TE is defensible:** PFF — *"higher correlation between fantasy points and PFF grades at tight end than other positions"*; McBride, Bowers, Kittle all 90+ receiving grades with a big gap to the rest.
- **Timing is bimodal:** real drop-off after the **first ~7–8 TEs**. Either pay up for an elite, or wait — e.g., **George Kittle ~Rd 10–11** (highest ceiling in his tier, ⚠️ torn Achilles, expected back early season).

## Quarterback `high`
- **QB1 Josh Allen** (consensus; DraftSharks projects 440-pt ceiling, far ahead on value). **Lamar Jackson QB2**, **Drake Maye** rising top-5 (QB3, finished QB2 in 2025), **Joe Burrow** elite tier.
- **Strategy: WAIT.** Near-unanimous across PFF, Yahoo/Boone, DraftSharks, RotoWire — the position is deep with a large interchangeable middle tier (Daniels, Burrow, Herbert, Hurts...). Target your **first QB rounds 5–8**, or take **two QBs late**. Only pay up for a *true* elite (bimodal).
  - PFF: *"Waiting at QB and picking two QBs late should be a popular strategy."*
  - RotoWire: *"waiting until rounds 5-8 for your first quarterback is often optimal."*

## Bye Weeks `high`
- 2026 byes span **Weeks 5–14**; **Week 11 is the heaviest** — six teams off (**ATL, CLE, GB, LAR, NE, SEA**), hitting Nacua, JSN, Drake London.
- Tactic: *don't overreact in-draft, but don't stack too many same-position players on the same bye.*

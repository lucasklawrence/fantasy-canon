## ⚡ The plan in one line

Build an **RB/WR foundation early**, grab **one elite TE (Bowers/McBride) or punt-and-stream**, take
**both an RB and WR before Round 8**, then mine **QB / TE / DST in Rounds 8–11** and **WR to the final
pick**. 2026 evidence favors **RB-forward builds** (Hero/Robust — early-RB hit rate ~70–75%, Zero-RB
edge eroded); Zero-RB is conditional, not a creed. _`tmpl` `strat` `end`_

## ⚡ Takeaway

**Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR, ADP 125.8, Round 11)** is the best-supported late-round target on the board — Dowdle gone, Hubbard 41st of 51 in yards per carry, Brooks fully cleared off his ACL. And a buried lede worth chasing: **ESPN's depth chart lists Zach Charbonnet as Seattle's starter ahead of Jadarian Price**, which if true makes Charbonnet at Round 13 drastically underpriced and turns him from an IR stash into a target.

## (A) Post-hype sleepers with a real path to volume

**Jonathon Brooks — RB, CAR — ADP 125.8 (Rd 11).** `confidence: high · 3-0`
The cleanest case in the set. Rico Dowdle left in free agency (corroborated on ESPN's depth chart: Hubbard starter, Brooks second, Etienne third, Dowdle absent). Chuba Hubbard ranked **41st of 51** RBs with 90+ carries in yards per carry. Brooks is "fully cleared and close to 100%" with strong June minicamp reviews, and FantasyPros independently reported (July 17) he "should enter camp fully healthy" after logging 11-on-11 work six weeks prior. Dave Canales' lead back has finished top-17 in PPR points per game three years running. DraftSharks places him in Tier 7 "committee backs with upside" — a tier _above_ its "an injury away" contingency group.
_Discount it for:_ a second tear of the same ACL and all of 2025 missed; he sits behind a starter drafted ~45 picks earlier. DraftSharks quotes "Round 9 or 10" against FFC's 125.8 and says his ADP is rising — **125.8 may already be stale.**

**Jalen McMillan — WR, TB — ADP ~164 (Rd 14).** `confidence: high · 3-0`
The cleanest _quantified_ vacancy: Mike Evans left for San Francisco having commanded a **25.8% target share** when healthy. McMillan posted 26/357/7 on a 19.3% share over his final six games as a 2024 rookie before a preseason neck injury erased 2025. Every number was verified against sources independent of the originating outlet (StatMuse game logs, ESPN, NFL.com), and the two target shares are arithmetically consistent against the same ~30 targets/game team denominator.
_Discount it for:_ 7 TDs on 26 catches is a **27% TD rate and not repeatable** — the closest thing in this set to a spike-week warning. The vacated targets are heavily contested by Godwin, Egbuka and Tez Johnson. A competing framing of Evans' vacancy as "1,088 yards and 9.0 TDs per season over 12 seasons" was **refuted 0-3** — use the target-share version only.

**Kyler Murray — QB, MIN — DraftSharks Rd 13 slot.** `confidence: medium · 3-0`
RotoWire's depth chart already lists Murray QB1 ahead of J.J. McCarthy, and ESPN reported he took first snaps in team drills at minicamp. DraftSharks recommends him at Round 13 across all four draft-slot guides, expecting him to win the job.
_Discount it for:_ Kevin O'Connell publicly **declined to shed light on the competition** (July 9) — this is projection, not reported camp fact. And "Round 13" is DraftSharks' own recommendation slot, **not a measured ADP.**

**D.J. Moore — WR, BUF — price unknown.** `confidence: medium · 2-1`
Confirmed WR1 starter in Buffalo on both ESPN's depth chart and NFL.com's roster, arriving off career lows. **But no ADP was obtained in any format**, so the "price collapsed" half of the post-hype thesis is unverified — he cannot yet be called a value or even confirmed available after pick 100. _Method trap:_ `fantasypros.com/nfl/players/dj-moore.php` returns a **different D.J. Moore**, a 39-year-old cornerback. Auto-pulling ADP from that slug yields garbage.

**The "cheap because contingent" bucket.** `confidence: medium · 3-0`
DraftSharks files eleven fallen-ADP WRs under a tier literally named **"The what ifs"** — Godwin, Josh Downs, Worthy, Diggs, Golden, Wan'Dale Robinson, Michael Wilson, Higgins, Lemon, Doubs, Concepcion — with the verdict "you don't want to be banking on any of these guys to carry your fantasy squad." Three qualifiers travel with that: the very next sentence is "but they all have the upside to be difference-makers" (it's a bucket label, not an avoid list); Diggs is a **free agent**, so his contingency is categorically different; and the tier carries **no ADP**, so it can't establish that any of them is available after pick 100.

## (C) Endgame roster construction

**Zach Charbonnet is the decisive IR test case — and possibly mispriced entirely.** `confidence: high · 3-0`
FFC redraft ADP **155.8 (Round 13)**, ranked the **#2 overall RB handcuff**, but that ranking explicitly assumes health and he's recovering from a Divisional-Round ACL tear. **With your 2 IR slots he costs no active roster spot — take him.** Four live qualifications: July reporting trends _more_ optimistic than the May source ("light work," "ahead of typical recovery schedule," late-July knee checkup); FantasyPros lists him ~20 picks earlier at #135, exactly the best-ball inflation of an injured upside stash you'd expect — **use 155.8**; FFC's player page shows "13.12 (155th overall)," the documented truncate-and-relabel trap.

**And the fourth one is the story:** ESPN's depth chart lists **Charbonnet as the STARTER ahead of Jadarian Price** — directly against the framing of Price (73.8) as lead back. If ESPN is right, a Round 13 pick is drastically underpriced and the IR lens is the wrong one entirely. **Resolve this before drafting.**

**Ceiling over floor — one outlet, no evidence.** `confidence: medium · 2-1`
DraftSharks prescribes upside darts and names a list (Cam Ward, Tyrone Tracy, Kaytron Allen, Jonah Coleman, MarShawn Lloyd, Jaydon Blue, Travis Hunter, Denzel Boston, Adonai Mitchell, Antonio Williams, Tre Harris, Chig Okonkwo). Three things cap it: it says "in Round 14," **singular** — one dart round, with 15-16 reserved for streaming K/DST, so this is not an open-ended dart endgame; it's **half-PPR**, so the receiving-heavy names shift up modestly in full PPR; and the operative instruction ("sort by projected ceiling in your Draft War Room") is a **paid-tool upsell**. The page carries no percentages or historical success rates of any kind. **Area C asked for hit-rate evidence and this source supplies none** — cite it as one shop's opinion, never as evidence that ceiling beats floor.

**Don't spend a late bench spot on a backup TE.** `confidence: medium · 2-1`
DraftSharks' lowest TE tier is named **"Waiver wire speed dial"** — Juwan Johnson, Okonkwo, Hockenson, Schultz, Gadsden — "not worth drafting in most lineup-setting fantasy leagues." All five price Rounds 13-15 on the FFC redraft board (Hockenson 149.2, Johnson 151.0, Gadsden 162.2, Okonkwo 167.6, Schultz 169.4). _Internal tension:_ the same article calls Gadsden the tier's best difference-maker chance, and Okonkwo appears on the same outlet's Round 14 dart list — a direct self-contradiction.

## Method finding (applies to every ADP figure we cite)

`confidence: high` · verified by direct self-fetch of the primary page (275,544 bytes, 2026-07-20)

**FantasyPros' PPR consensus ADP page cannot be used as a 12-team redraft board**, for three independent reasons:

1. **Sources:** footer reads "ADP Sources: BB10, Sleeper, Drafters." Drafters.com was fetched directly — its formats are Best Ball, Pick'em and Condensed Season, **no traditional redraft**. BB10 is BestBall10s, and critically those are **10-team, 20-round** drafts, so their pick numbers map badly onto our board on two axes at once.
2. **Column trap:** schema confirmed verbatim — a `rank` integer field structurally separate from a decimal `avg` field, replicating the FFC trap. **Aggravating detail: at ranks 3-5 the avg equals the rank exactly** (Chase 3/3.0, Nacua 4/4.0, JSN 5/5.0), so a top-5 sanity check would **fail to detect** column substitution. Spot-checks must use deep players.
3. **Registration fence:** the page serves only 5 rows unauthenticated. The entire 90-200 ADP band this question lives in is unobtainable there.
